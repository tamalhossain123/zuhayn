About Us

The Agency Behind Ambitious Brands

ZUHAYN — Creative & IT Solutions was founded in 2020 in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a simple idea: small and growing businesses deserve the same quality of design and marketing as the big brands — without the big-agency complexity.

Today we help entrepreneurs and businesses present themselves professionally and grow online through digital marketing, creative and graphic design, branding, video editing and web design — working with clients in Bangladesh and abroad.
